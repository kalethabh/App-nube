    THE PROBLEM

    The early signs of cardiovascular disease

    Stress and modern food keep your body a little inflamed all the time. The older you get, the more that matters. Watch which steps come first, because they're not the ones you'd expect.

    1

    Inflammation

    This is the spark. Low-level inflammation is what starts the damage inside your arteries, long before anything else shows up. In about half of all heart attacks, cholesterol looked totally fine.

    Ridker PM et al. New England Journal of Medicine, 1997.

    2

    Weak Vessel Lining

    The lining inside your blood vessels slowly stops making nitric oxide, the stuff that tells your vessels to relax and open. This is the very first thing doctors can actually measure.

    Bonetti PO et al. ATVB, 2003.

    3

    High Blood Pressure

    72% of people who stop their blood pressure pills are back to high numbers within 6 months. The pill hides the problem, but it never fixes the weak lining causing it.

    Whelton PK et al. JACC, 2018.

    4

    High Cholesterol

    By the time a test shows high cholesterol, plaque has been building up for 10-20 years. A pill lowers the number, but it doesn't repair the lining that let the cholesterol in.

    Libby P et al. Nature Reviews Disease Primers, 2019.
